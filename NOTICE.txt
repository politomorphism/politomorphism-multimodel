  This repository does not contain the text of the CoCoLoFa corpus (Yeh, Wan & Huang,
  EMNLP 2024; github.com/Crowd-AI-Lab/cocolofa). It contains only comment ids, labels

  The corpus has its own terms and its authors ask to be cited; check their repository
  before using or redistributing the corpus. The writer ids are pseudonymous numbers

  affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Intended use and limits
  The code and results are for research on detecting a reasoning pattern in text. They
  describe the behavior of language models on one corpus under one prompt. They must
  not be used to judge, rank, sanction or profile individual writers or commenters, and
  they are not validated for moderation, legal, employment or other decisions about
  people.
